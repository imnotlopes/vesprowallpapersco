Your screen deserves a painting

Thanks for joining us. Think about how many hours your eyes rest on a screen. We wanted it to feel closer to a painting on a wall, so we started painting wallpapers.

Why impasto

Impasto is the old technique of laying paint on so thick that it keeps the shape of the brush. It has ridges, edges and small happy accidents. On a bright, flat screen, that texture gives your eye somewhere to rest.

Made for your screen

Each painting is composed for a 16:9 screen first, so nothing important hides behind your icons, your dock or your menu bar.
